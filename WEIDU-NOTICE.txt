binary, downloaded from the WeiDU maintainers' official release:
https://github.com/WeiDUorg/weidu/releases/tag/v251.00

and contributors. The accompanying WEIDU-COPYING.txt contains its license.

WeiDU documentation permits redistribution of unmodified binary copies
alongside mods: https://weidu.org/~thebigg/README-WeiDU.html

The mod source in fixed-lighting is a custom prototype for this request.
